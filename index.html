About

What I do

I lead WordPress VIP development on a multisite network of hundreds of client properties, including SportsEngine and GolfNow. Custom Gutenberg blocks from scratch in React and TypeScript. No ACF, no page builders. That's a deliberate architectural choice: real blocks are faster, testable, and nicer for editors.

I treat the editor as the primary user. If the editing experience is clunky, the block isn't done.
